⚠ /test is hard-blocked in production launchers (CurseForge, Prism, MultiMC, vanilla, dedicated server). NeoForge's GameTestHooks.isGametestEnabled() checks !FMLLoader.isProduction() first, so the -Dneoforge.enableGameTest=true JVM arg has zero effect on a shipped jar. There is no supported way to expose /test in your normal modpack. /test est bloqué en dur dans les launchers de production (CurseForge, Prism, MultiMC, vanilla, serveur dédié).
